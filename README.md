🛒 EcomLens – Business at a Glance

A Tableau dashboard that shows how an online store is doing: its sales, customers, products, deliveries and returns.

🔗 Live dashboard: Tableau Public link - https://public.tableau.com/views/Ecommerce_17907808924450/HomeDashboard?:language=en-GB&:sid=&:redirect=auth&publish=yes&showOnboarding=true&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link

📌 About the Project

An e-commerce company wanted a simple way to see how its business is performing. I analyzed 6,000 orders and built a set of dashboards to answer questions like:
<br>
Which products and regions sell the most?<br>
Who are the best customers, and who stopped buying?<br>
Which products get returned the most?<br>
How fast are orders delivered?<br>
How do customers prefer to pay?<br><br>

📂 Dataset<br>
6,000 orders from June 2024 to June 2026<br>
23 columns, including order, customer, product, sales, profit, region, payment, return and delivery details<br><br>

🛠 Tools Used<br>
Tableau: to build the charts and dashboards<br>
Calculated fields & LOD expressions: to create new measures like delivery days and customer scores<br>
Parameters & filters: so users can switch between monthly, quarterly and yearly views<br><br>

🔄 Steps Followed<br>
Loaded the data into Tableau<br>
Checked and cleaned the data<br>
Created new calculated fields<br>
Built the charts<br>
Combined the charts into dashboards<br><br>

📊 Dashboards<br>
Dashboard	What it shows<br>
Home	Main numbers: revenue, orders, customers, average order value<br>
Sales Analysis	Sales over time, by category and by region<br>
Customer Insights	Best customers, new vs. returning customers, customers who stopped buying<br>
Product Performance	Best-selling products, most returned products, weak products<br>
Delivery Analysis	Delivery time and each shipping partner's performance<br>
Payment & Returns	Payment methods and reasons for returns<br><br>

💡 Key Findings<br>
Total revenue: ₹4.76 Cr, with ₹1.42 Cr profit<br>
Electronics is the best-selling category<br>
South is the top region<br>
About 70% of customers bought only once<br>
Delivery takes about 8 days on average, and more than half of orders take longer than a week<br>
Damaged items is the top reason for returns<br><br>

✅ Suggestions<br>
Speed up deliveries by working with the shipping partners<br>
Use better packaging to reduce damaged returns<br>
Offer discounts and rewards to bring back one-time buyers<br><br>
