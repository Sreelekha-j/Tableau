🛒 EcomLens – Business at a Glance

A Tableau dashboard that shows how an online store is doing: its sales, customers, products, deliveries and returns.

🔗 Live dashboard: Tableau Public link - https://public.tableau.com/views/Ecommerce_17907808924450/HomeDashboard?:language=en-GB&:sid=&:redirect=auth&publish=yes&showOnboarding=true&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link

📌 About the Project

An e-commerce company wanted a simple way to see how its business is performing. I analyzed 6,000 orders and built a set of dashboards to answer questions like:

Which products and regions sell the most?
Who are the best customers, and who stopped buying?
Which products get returned the most?
How fast are orders delivered?
How do customers prefer to pay?
📂 Dataset
6,000 orders from June 2024 to June 2026
23 columns, including order, customer, product, sales, profit, region, payment, return and delivery details
🛠 Tools Used
Tableau: to build the charts and dashboards
Calculated fields & LOD expressions: to create new measures like delivery days and customer scores
Parameters & filters: so users can switch between monthly, quarterly and yearly views
🔄 Steps Followed
Loaded the data into Tableau
Checked and cleaned the data
Created new calculated fields
Built the charts
Combined the charts into dashboards
📊 Dashboards
Dashboard	What it shows
Home	Main numbers: revenue, orders, customers, average order value
Sales Analysis	Sales over time, by category and by region
Customer Insights	Best customers, new vs. returning customers, customers who stopped buying
Product Performance	Best-selling products, most returned products, weak products
Delivery Analysis	Delivery time and each shipping partner's performance
Payment & Returns	Payment methods and reasons for returns

💡 Key Findings
Total revenue: ₹4.76 Cr, with ₹1.42 Cr profit
Electronics is the best-selling category
South is the top region
About 70% of customers bought only once
Delivery takes about 8 days on average, and more than half of orders take longer than a week
Damaged items is the top reason for returns
✅ Suggestions
Speed up deliveries by working with the shipping partners
Use better packaging to reduce damaged returns
Offer discounts and rewards to bring back one-time buyers
